# Candid Therapeutics

> Clinical-stage biotechnology company in San Diego, California developing T-cell engager (TCE) antibody therapies for autoimmune and inflammatory diseases. Launched September 2024 with a $370M Series A. Acquired by UCB, completed 17 June 2026, for up to $2.2 billion. Candid Therapeutics publishes no public API, developer portal, SDK, or machine-readable specification.

## Science

- T-cell engager (TCE) bispecific antibodies that redirect T-cells to selectively deplete autoreactive B-cells, targeting deeper and more durable depletion than conventional antibody therapy without the manufacturing burden of cell therapy.
- Lead investigational asset: cizutamig, a BCMA x CD3 bispecific antibody.
- Pipeline assembled by acquiring Vignette Bio and TRC 2004, in-licensing differentiated TCE assets for US clinical development.
